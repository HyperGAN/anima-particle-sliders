# Post-train the Final Boss slider for less image damage

Fork one trained Anima RAW192 slider twice. Give each fork 48 additional stock ParticleGAN updates. The control continues on the parent’s frozen training targets. The treatment sees cleaner Boss targets on half the training scenes. Compare both children with each other and with the untouched parent. This is a proposed experiment. The checkpoint fork described below still needs implementation.

**Use the same parent for both arms.** Start with the Vibe12 background-projection adapter, SHA-256 `dfda230137f352346cf9349adea8d92b20c1fdf263c75650fdeb349a4f86f81c`. Under the previous paired-image damage rubric on eight development images, early α 1.0 produced **+71.50 Boss gain, 38.79 damage and 12.50 final score**. Constant α 1.4375 produced **+72.25 gain, 41.08 damage and 10.69 score**. All values use a 0–100 scale. Re-evaluate the parent using the same current single-image damage rubric as the children before applying the proposed tolerances. These are two settings of the same trained network. A post-training win should improve on what changing the parent’s strength already gives us. ## Change the targets

Freeze six of the original twelve training scenes as anchors. For each of the other six, render the parent edit and two or three alternative Boss prompts from the same neutral prompt, seed and native sampler. Vary the character’s costume and silhouette while keeping the setting and light fixed in the prompt. Ask Jev for character Boss gain over OFF and graded image damage. As an initial teacher rule, select the least damaged alternative whose gain matches or exceeds the parent’s and whose damage is lower. Keep the original training target wherever no alternative meets both conditions. Record which scenes actually changed. If none qualify, improve the proposals before spending the training budget.

Rebuild each accepted pair into ten native timesteps on each of the neutral and positive trajectories. At every stored latent and timestep, evaluate both frozen base-model velocities with adapters disabled, then apply the parent recipe’s field transformation. Pin the prompts, seeds, teacher images, fields and cache identity, and verify native replay. Keep 240 rows and equal scene exposure in both arms. Jev chooses the teacher. The trained adapter receives the original generator gradient through the field critic, which compares Gaussian real samples with noise shifted by the student–teacher velocity residual. This requires no differentiable image judge or additional GAN loss. [Native field capture and verification](https://github.com/HyperGAN/anima-particle-sliders/blob/5981336de4af939e430d0940659cfddb723a1023/release_tools/final_boss_targets.py) · [Anima response rows](https://github.com/HyperGAN/anima-particle-sliders/blob/5981336de4af939e430d0940659cfddb723a1023/lumen_studio/engine_training.py)

If the cleaner-prompt arm cannot retain effect, a narrower follow-up would replace the teacher velocities only at the final three denoising positions of each trajectory and preserve the original targets at the other seven. Test this as its own arm, with the same parent and control.

## Decide whether post-training helped

Hold learning rates, batch size, seed, sampler and budget fixed between children. Record checkpoints at +12, +24, +36 and +48. Compare both +48 RAWs to the parent. Give all three the same alpha search starting at 1.0 with a 0.25 step, up to nine probes for each of constant, early and late schedules, the same four screening cases and eight development cases. Select by the damage-adjusted Final Boss score. Keep the strongest-effect finalist visible and save each schedule’s actual per-step gains.

Lock each selected strength before rendering **eight fresh confirmation scenes × two seeds**, disjoint from teachers, screening and development. Report paired changes in gain, damage and final score against both controls, along with matched images. Plot gain against damage across the measured development strengths to expose gains available from alpha tuning alone. A strict Pareto improvement lowers damage and preserves or increases effect. For a practical pilot win, predeclare a tolerance, for example mean Boss gain no more than two points lower, damage at least five points lower and a higher final score versus **both** controls. Show scene-level variation. An improvement over the parent alone can come from the extra 48 updates. The equal-update control measures that contribution.

For an effect-matched comparison, choose the lowest-damage measured setting within a predeclared ±2 gain points of the parent on development, and freeze that operating point too. Report a missed band explicitly. Keep both approved operating points fixed on confirmation. Average paired seed differences within each scene and report uncertainty across the eight scenes.
