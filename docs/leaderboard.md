# Anima Final Boss leaderboard

## Summary

**Current research default.** Soft foreground weighting with a 0.45 background floor, trained to RAW192 and replayed at early α 0.95. It leads the saved replays with **14.72** on eight development cases and scores **10.64** on eight separate confirmation cases. The original score was 33.71 on development and 28.55 on confirmation. [Default and exact gains](final-boss-raw192/default.json) · [Matched confirmation images](final-boss-raw192/confirmed-default.jpg) · [Download RAW checkpoints](https://huggingface.co/ntc-ai/anima-final-boss-research)

**What worked in training.** Soft foreground weighting concentrates the teacher edit on the character while retaining some signal in the background. It leads at shared early strength with **6.63**. Full-strength Boss cues on the same background floor score **5.57** at shared early strength and lead at shared constant strength with **2.10**. [Training comparison](#training-techniques) · [Target-generation source](final-boss-raw192/README.md#source-for-the-trained-targets)

**What earns a high score.** The finished character's Boss distinction is cubed and multiplied by the positive gain over the original. Image damage reduces the score, with a steeper penalty above Jev's minor-defect anchor. [Scoring and comparison protocol](#comparing-a-new-run) · [Jev rubrics](final-boss-raw192/rubrics.json)

**Overnight challenger.** Paired scene and lighting contexts at a 0.45 floor finished at 00:15 UTC on November 24 and reached **10.44** at constant α 1.4125. At shared early strength, it scored **3.63**. Middle exposure later finished at **2.10** shared early and **0.09** shared constant. ## Second noise seed on the shared teacher set

Concept Sliders draws fresh initial noise at every training iteration. Native Anima training instead replays a frozen cache of teacher fields captured at one noise seed per scene. This trial adds a second seed to that cache to give the adapter more varied training states. It uses the shared teacher set selected on main: 5 scenes × 20 fields at seed 31237, taken verbatim from `prepare_targets`. In each scene with a qualified second seed, the neutral trajectory's odd positions and the positive trajectory's even positions come from that seed. Seeds 31238, 31239 and 31240 were tried in that fixed order. A seed qualified under main's `qualifies(row, 0.75)` rule and a second judge read. Knight, tree and colossus took 31238, insect took 31239, and wyrm kept its original fields, so 40 of the 100 fields come from a second seed. Training used stock ParticleGAN EMPF updates with seed 1, batch 2, 192 updates, lr 0.00075, `d_lr_mult` 12, `prior_lr_mult` 40, RAW weights and `ema_decay=None`. [Concept Sliders](https://www.ecva.net/papers/eccv_2024/papers_ECCV/papers/05660.pdf) · [Reference trainer](https://github.com/rohitgandikota/sliders/blob/main/trainscripts/textsliders/train_lora.py) · [Cache, gate and per-case scores](final-boss-raw192/ablations/shared-seed-split-raw192-scores.json)

The matched control is the stock run on the same 100 unsplit fields from the separate shared-teacher study, with the same options, initialization seed, prompts, sampler and gains. It isolates the seed crossing. Its readings are cited unchanged from that study. The slider-off Boss readings are identical in both arms. The control's early α 2/3 setting is unmeasured in its own study because Jev left violinist's structural check unresolved and no Boss readings were recorded, so there is no matched early comparison.

Under the current leaderboard score, the seed split scored **0.02** at shared early α 2/3 and **0.01** at shared constant α 0.6375. The stock control scored **0.01** at constant strength (0.0133 versus 0.0138 before rounding). Mean Boss distinction was **0.143** early and **0.140** constant for the seed split and **0.129** for stock at constant, against **0.081** with the slider off. ## Percentile-filtered teacher edits

The Anima training adaptation blends 35% of the norm-matched top 10% of the soft-foreground edit into the dense edit, then restores the original per-field L2 norm. A separate CUDA capture using published source reproduced all **240** teacher fields bit for bit, and both captures passed native admission. Both fits completed 192 updates. Compare the saved RAW checkpoint with the incumbent on the same prompts, seeds, sampler, and shared strengths. At 192 updates the early score to beat is **6.63**. The full-strength Boss cue recipe sets the constant-strength comparison at **2.10**.

When a training recipe wins at shared early strength, choose its alpha and schedule using the development cases.
